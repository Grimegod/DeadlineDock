1. Evaluation
An organisation may evaluate this version for 30 days from workspace creation.
Afterwards reading, CSV export and backup remain available. A valid organisation
licence enables continued changes.

2. Price and grant
The publisher's total software licence price is at most EUR 10 for one named
organisation, paid once, without subscription or per-seat charges. After purchase
confirmation and delivery of a signed licence, the organisation receives a
permanent, non-exclusive right to install, back up and use the purchased major
version internally on any number of its workstations, with any number of internal
users. There is no device activation quota. Version 0.2.1 belongs to major 0.

3. Organisation scope
One organisation means one named legal entity, including departments/branches.
Contractors may access its workspace on its behalf for its internal work.
Separate incorporated companies, agencies and subsidiaries are separate
organisations, each requiring its own licence. Resale, sublicensing and hosted
service provision to unrelated organisations are not included. Contact the
publisher for a licence transfer after a merger or change of legal entity.

acknowledgement is not completion of an underlying obligation. Do not use the
program as the sole safeguard for critical deadlines.

A licence identifies the organisation and its workspace organisation ID. It has
